## Observed Patterns

### Worked (D)

- **"What changed" narrative-lead** (5-of-5 [fictional]): customers consistently described the lead paragraph as the context that made the actions trustable [fictional].
- **Sub-90-sec comprehension** (5-of-5 [fictional]; median 1:10 [fictional]): well below 5-min target.
- **Action bullets with SKU + store + qty specificity** (5-of-5 [fictional]): self-described all 3 as actionable [fictional].

### Hesitated (A)

- **Named-analyst subject-line** (3-of-5 [fictional]): customers either didn't know Carlos personally (C2, C3) or found the subject-line cleverness manipulative (C4) [fictional].
