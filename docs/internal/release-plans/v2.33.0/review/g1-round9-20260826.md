### R9-F1 (high) - the precondition proves the wrong half of the pipeline

> The shared precondition proves only that one benign trace reached the collector before testing. It
> never proves that the durable writer is live, and neither Recovery step sends a fresh allowed
> control. With the collector up and storage down, sentinel B reaches the collector and remains
> absent from storage even if the normal storage minimizer is fail-open.

**CONFIRMED.** The D20 rewrite made both blocks symmetric and gave them a **shared** control that
only exercises the egress path. A storage-side control was never added, and the recovery steps
recheck absence without re-establishing that anything can still be written. Symmetry of *shape* was
achieved; equivalence of *strength* was not, which is precisely what round 9 was asked to attack.

### R9-F2 (high) - the sentinel definition excludes the common case

> The policy rows allow stripping or masking within a captured class, but both sentinels and both
> Not-applicable branches are defined only around entire forbidden classes. If user text may cross
> after emails or secrets are masked, no class is wholly forbidden, so the spec can declare egress
> untested even though a real minimizer exists.

**CONFIRMED, and it is the more serious of the two.** Whole-class prohibition is the *rarer* real
configuration; value-level masking within a permitted class is the common one. As written, the
common case routes straight to "Not applicable" and a broken masker passes by construction.
