### R6-F1 (Kano method versus adequacy) - high, fix-pass damage

> **Kano evidence tiers contradict the sample-size fix**
> (`skills/define-prioritization-framework/SKILL.md:155`)
> The new refusal says a handful of responses earns the inferred tier, but lines 209-210 define tiers
> solely by collection method: any formal Kano question pairs are surveyed and their categories are
> directly measured. An agent can therefore classify the same small formal survey as surveyed,
> preserving the overclaim this commit intended to remove.
> Recommendation: Separate collection method from sample adequacy. Label small formal surveys as
> surveyed but direction-only, prohibit directly measured language until explicit adequacy conditions
> are met, and enforce both dimensions in the checklist and template.

**CONFIRMED.** The `4f5d79ed` fix asserted that a small sample lands in the **inferred** tier, while
the tier table defines tiers purely by **collection method**: formal Kano pairs are surveyed and
"categories are directly measured" regardless of n. The fix conflated two independent dimensions and
left the overclaim reachable by the surveyed route.

### R6-F2 (retry safety) - high, fix-pass damage

> **Forced storage-failure test is not retry-safe**
> (`skills/measure-instrumentation-spec/references/TEMPLATE.md:173`)
> The test proves sentinel B reached the collector and was initially absent from durable sinks, but
> it defines no terminal disposition or retry horizon. An asynchronous collector can buffer the
> trace, pass this check while the fault is active, then persist the forbidden data after recovery or
> replay.
> Recommendation: Require the failure path to drop the trace permanently. Exercise fault recovery and
> replay, drain or advance all retry paths, and assert B remains absent from queues, dead-letter
> stores, and final sinks through the defined retry horizon.

**CONFIRMED as a real gap.** A point-in-time absence assertion cannot distinguish "dropped" from
"buffered and not yet written". **Flagged for maintainer judgment on remedy depth:** requiring a PM
instrumentation template to specify dead-letter drainage and retry horizons may exceed what a
PM-authored artifact should carry. A lighter closure that names the **terminal disposition** as a
required decision, without enumerating queue mechanics, may serve the same contract. That is a
design call, not a factual dispute.

### R6-F3 (market sizing) - high, fix-pass damage

> **Partial-install fallback does not compute market size**
> (`skills/foundation-build-risk-review/SKILL.md:90`)
> The new fallback calls reachable accounts times attach rate a bottom-up market estimate. Its result
> is a customer count, not market value. The destination skill defines bottom-up sizing as customer
> count times revenue per customer.

**CONFIRMED.** `discover-market-sizing` produces TAM, SAM and SOM, which are monetary, and its input
list names a "revenue-per-customer assumption (improves bottom-up)". The fallback row added in the
R5-F6 fix omits the revenue factor entirely, so it yields a count and labels it a market estimate.
**A fallback that teaches the wrong calculation is worse than the bare pointer it replaced.**

### R6-F4 (fabricated bias correction) - high, pre-existing since v2.18.0

> **Survey skill invents a 20-40 point bias correction**
> (`skills/measure-survey-analysis/SKILL.md:144`)
> The refusal tells agents that a leading question is likely overstated by 20-40 percentage points
> based on unspecified research. No source, instrument comparison, population, or derivation supports
> that range, and leading-question effects are context-dependent.

**CONFIRMED, and it is the sharpest pre-existing finding of the cycle.** `git log -S` dates the line
to `c763bc3c`, the v2.18.0 content-skill batch. **The skill whose entire contract is refusing
fabricated numbers ships a fabricated number in its own refusal text**, and it has shipped that way
for five releases. Same class as D15.

### R6-F5 (tiny samples as gates) - high, pre-existing, OUTSIDE the diff

> **Opportunity example uses tiny samples as pass/fail evidence**
> (`skills/define-opportunity-tree/references/EXAMPLE.md:182-185`)
> The completed example treats 4 of 5 prototype participants as greater than 70 percent completion

**CONFIRMED as a defect and CONFIRMED as out of scope.** `define-opportunity-tree` is not in

### R6-F6 (design of record contradicts shipped contracts) - medium, pre-existing

> **Design of record still calls the required contract optional and minor**
> (`docs/internal/release-plans/v2.32.0/spec_c3-ai-product-family.md:55-60`)
> The AI-family spec remains described as the design of record, yet its increment table still labels
> Agent Execution Contract optional and all four increments minor. A future implementation pass
> following this spec can reintroduce the exact contract error round 5 fixed.

**CONFIRMED.** Four rows read `MINOR` and one says `Optional`, against D11's retype to three MAJORs
and R5-F2's correction to `CONDITIONAL`. A standing design-of-record that contradicts the shipped
contract will re-seed the defect on the next build pass.
